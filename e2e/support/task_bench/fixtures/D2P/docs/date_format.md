# Date format

A date is written `YYYY-MM-DD`: a four-digit year, then the month and the day, each zero-padded to
two digits and separated by hyphens. When a time is given it follows a `T` as `HH:MM`, in 24-hour
UTC, zero-padded: `2026-09-28T07:05`. A time in another zone is converted to UTC first; seconds are
never written.
